## CONTENT RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE - TRC standard)
- **NO hyphens as punctuation, NO em dashes ( - ), NO en dashes (–).** Use "to" for ranges ("$1,500 to $3,000", "Monday to Saturday"). Hyphenated compound words (blown-in, closed-cell) are fine.
- NO AI filler ("in today's world", "let's dive in", "when it comes to", "navigating").
- Short punchy sentences. Active voice. Max 1 exclamation mark per piece.
- Keywords woven naturally, never stuffed. Phone/email CTAs throughout.
- Bilingual angle emphasized. **NEVER invent reviews/testimonials/credentials** (company is newer; honesty is the brand - see soul.md).
- Never publish TRC/agency pricing.

## BLOG STRUCTURE (every post)
- Frontmatter: title, description, date, author, category, cover.
- Body: `## Quick answer` -> H2 sections -> `## Key takeaways` -> `## Frequently asked questions` -> CTA with phone.
- A comparison/cost table somewhere (AEO citation magnet). Minimum 3 internal links. 4+ of the 6 GEO patterns.
- Post layout: left sticky social share, right sticky jump-to-section, related posts below, author bio box (E-E-A-T).

## AUTOMATION / SEO OPS (standard infra)
- `scripts/daily-blog.py` + `.github/workflows/daily-blog.yml` - monthly SEO post via Gemini (cron `0 16 1 * *`). Needs repo secret `GEMINI_API_KEY` (TRC-shared) and optional `GCP_SA_KEY_JSON` for sheet logging. Reads `.seo/context.json` + `.seo/internal-links.json`.
- `scripts/leads-sync.py` + `.github/workflows/leads-sync.yml` - Web3Forms leads -> Google Sheet, every 15 min. Needs `GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD` + `GCP_SA_KEY_JSON`. Set `SHEET_ID` once the H&S leads sheet exists.
- `scripts/seo-auto-fix/index.mjs` + `.github/workflows/seo-auto-fix.yml` - weekly schema/SEO auto-fixer driven by `seo-config.json`.
- `scripts/generate-link-library.mjs` + `scripts/generate-llms-txt.mjs` - run on every `npm run build` (prebuild) to refresh `.seo/internal-links.json` and `public/llms.txt`.
