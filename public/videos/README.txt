HERO BACKGROUND VIDEO
=====================
Drop your looping 3D insulation animation here and it plays automatically as the
hero background. No code change needed.

Required filename(s):
  hero-insulation.mp4    (H.264, required)
  hero-insulation.webm   (VP9, optional but smaller/sharper - nice to have)

Recommended specs:
  - 1920x1080 (16:9), or wider. It is object-cover cropped, so center the action.
  - Seamless loop (first and last frame match).
  - 6 to 12 seconds.
  - Muted / no audio (it autoplays muted).
  - Keep under ~6-8 MB if possible (compress; it loads on every homepage visit).
  - Darker / lower-contrast footage reads best behind the white headline. The
    site adds a dark gradient over it for legibility either way.

Until a file is here, the hero shows the poster image
(public/images/hero/hero-attic-sprayfoam.jpg) with animated insulation waves.
